01
The dashboard was beautiful. The operator was blind.
A field service operator had a polished dashboard. Every metric displayed. She couldn't tell in 3 seconds whether her business was healthy or failing. The design served itself. Not her.
02
$49 a month. Seven tools. No intelligence.
ChatGPT for copy. Midjourney for images. Canva for brand. Jasper for tone. Each tool ran independently. The outputs contradicted each other. The business looked fragmented because the tools were.
03
The brand was impressive. Nobody knew what it was for.
Beautiful typography. Refined palette. A hero that explained the philosophy at length. Visitors decoded the system instead of feeling the value. The trunk was hidden behind its own branches.
